Custom Portfolio Website for Boris Hnatjuk — Photographer

Web Design Web Development Client Portal Photography

About the Client

Boris Hnatjuk is a professional photographer based in Croatia, known for his striking black-and-white work and architectural photography. His portfolio spans multiple disciplines — from fine art projects to commissioned architectural documentation.

As his body of work grew, Boris needed a digital home that matched the quality and precision of his photography: clean, purposeful, and built to serve both the public and his private clients.

Our Solution

We designed and built a custom portfolio website that serves two distinct audiences simultaneously. For the public, it presents Boris's work in a gallery format with a minimal, high-contrast aesthetic that never competes with the photography. For private clients, it provides a secure, password-protected access area where they can review and select images from their sessions.

The site is fully bilingual — Croatian and English — reflecting Boris's work across local and international markets.

Key Features
🔐
Private Client Portal
Clients receive a unique access code to enter a private area where they can browse their photos and mark the ones they wish to select. Boris receives a consolidated list of their choices, streamlining the selection process entirely.
🗂️
Self-Managed Categories
Boris can independently add and manage project categories and tabs — such as Architecture, ICM Architecture, and others — directly through the site's admin interface, without needing developer support.
🎨
4 Visual Themes
The website can be viewed in four distinct colour themes — pure white, light grey, dark grey, and black — allowing visitors to experience the photography in the context that suits them best. Boris controls the default theme.
