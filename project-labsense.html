Brand Identity and Marketing for Labsense — Laboratory Innovation

Logo Design Brand Identity Strategic Marketing Event Materials

About the Client

Labsense is a Croatian technology company specialising in customised laboratory solutions — from IoT sensor integration and laboratory management systems to process automation and digitalisation. Their tagline says it all: Make a difference. Choose customization.

Operating at the intersection of science and technology, Labsense needed a brand identity that communicated precision, trust, and innovation — positioned to make an impact on both domestic and international markets.

Our Work Together

Amelo Design developed the complete visual identity for Labsense from the ground up. The logo brings together four circles in a 2×2 grid — a mark that references laboratory wells, data points, and modular thinking, while remaining clean and highly versatile across every application.

The deep blue palette was chosen to convey technical authority and scientific credibility, setting Labsense apart in a sector where trust is paramount. The identity was designed to perform equally well in print, on screen, and in large-format applications.

From Brand to Stage

The identity was deployed across a full suite of conference and event materials — roll-up banners, table displays, brochures, and digital assets — giving Labsense a powerful and cohesive presence at industry events and international exhibitions.
