Logo Design for Botun Luxury Apartment — Inspired by Šibenik Heritage

Logo Design Brand Identity Luxury Heritage

About the Client

Botun Luxury Apartment is a premium accommodation in Šibenik, Croatia — a city renowned for its rich Dalmatian heritage and UNESCO-protected old town. The client sought a visual identity that would reflect both the luxury of the property and its deep connection to local culture.

The Design Concept

The logo draws direct inspiration from the Šibenik botun — a traditional piece of Dalmatian jewellery that has been crafted in the region for centuries. The botun is a rounded silver button with intricate filigree work, worn as a symbol of identity, pride, and craftsmanship.

We translated this heritage object into a contemporary logo mark — clean, elegant, and instantly recognisable to anyone familiar with the Dalmatian coast. The result bridges the old and the new: a symbol of local tradition presented through a modern luxury lens.

Application

The logo was designed for versatility across all brand touchpoints — from printed materials and signage to digital platforms. The gold and white colour variants ensure the mark performs beautifully on both light and dark backgrounds, maintaining its premium feel in every context.
