About SNP Group

SNP Group is an internationally recognised technology company specialising in software solutions and consulting services for complex business transformations. With a strong presence across Europe and beyond, SNP serves global enterprises navigating digital change at scale.

Amelo Design partnered with SNP Group to elevate their international visibility, strengthen key business relationships, and support their expansion into new markets through targeted strategic marketing initiatives.

Our Work Together

Our collaboration focused on developing and executing a comprehensive marketing strategy tailored to SNP's global ambitions. We worked closely with their leadership team to create a unified brand narrative that resonated with international audiences — from potential partners to institutional investors.

A key pillar of our engagement was the preparation and management of high-profile investor events. From concept to execution, we handled communications, presentation materials, and audience engagement strategies — ensuring every event reinforced SNP's position as a trusted global player.

We also supported SNP's efforts to build lasting partnerships on the international stage, crafting outreach campaigns and relationship-building strategies that opened doors to new markets and extended their network of collaborators.
